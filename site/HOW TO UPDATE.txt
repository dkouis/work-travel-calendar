WORK & TRAVEL CALENDAR — UPGRADE TO v2 (tasks, events, projects, reminders, reports)

WHAT TO UPLOAD
  Upload these files to the calendar folder on your server, replacing the old ones:
    index.php, api.php, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, icon.svg, icon-192.png,
    icon-512.png, .htaccess
  Do NOT upload or replace the data folder: it holds your live calendar and
  accounts.
  (Show hidden files in your FTP program so .htaccess is uploaded.)

BEFORE YOU UPLOAD (recommended)
  Sign in to the current calendar and use ⋯ → Export backup (.json).
  The server also keeps earlier versions (⋯ → Earlier versions).

FIRST OPEN
  Sign in as the editor. The calendar is upgraded automatically: your days,
  work status, locations, flights, comments and month notes stay exactly as
  they are, and projects, events, tasks and reminders become available.
  Viewers can keep using the old link; they see the new views read-only.

FIRST STEPS
  1. ⚙ Settings → General: check work hours (default 09:00–18:00, Mon–Fri).
  2. ⚙ Settings → Calendars & privacy: set a passphrase for Personal items.
     Personal items are encrypted in your browser; viewers only see "Busy".
     The passphrase cannot be recovered — keep it safe.
  3. ⚙ Settings → Reminders & notifications: turn on desktop notifications.
  4. Create projects with ＋ New → Project, or the ＋ next to WORK/PERSONAL.
  5. Try the quick-add box:  Meeting WG tue 10-12:30 #Council @Brussels
                             Draft text by fri 17:00 ~4h p1
  6. Drag tasks from the right panel onto the week grid to schedule them.
  Press ? in the app for all keyboard shortcuts.

REMINDERS
  Reminders pop up while the calendar is open in a browser tab (it can be in
  the background). To keep it running, install it as an app (Chrome/Edge:
  "Install app" in the address bar; iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen).
  Reminders missed while it was closed are shown the next time you open it.
  For alerts on your phone when the app is closed, export a .ics file
  (⋯ → Export calendar file) and import it into your phone's calendar.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Changes still save automatically ("Saved ✓"). If two editors save at the
    same time, the changes are combined; an item changed by both keeps the
    newer version and you see a note.
  - Ctrl+Z undoes the last change (up to 50). Deleted items stay in
    ⚙ Settings → Trash for 30 days.
  - The server accepts up to 3 MB of calendar data (several years of use).
    ⚙ Settings → Data → Archive moves old items to a file if it gets close.
  - Use https:// so passwords travel encrypted (also needed for the
    installable app).

FLIGHTS, HALF DAYS AND TIME ZONES (update 1 Nov 2026)
  - Active time zone: the 🌍 button at the top. New events, tasks and
    appointments are entered in it, and everything is shown in it. When you
    travel, switch it (e.g. to Brussels) and all items move to local time.
    Each item keeps the zone it was entered in, so nothing is lost. When your
    flights show you are in another city, the app offers to switch.
    The choice is per device and per user.
  - Flights appear as blocks in the Day and Week grids. Defaults, in the local
    time of the departure city:
      Morning 08:00–12:00, alarm 06:00
      Noon    16:00–20:00, alarm 14:00
      Night   20:00–24:00, alarm 17:30
    Open a flight's day to change its departure, arrival or alarm time, or to
    switch the alarm off. "↺ default" restores the defaults. Flights saved as
    "Evening" in the old app are shown as Noon.
  - The working day is Morning 08:30–13:00 and Evening 13:00–17:30
    (⚙ Settings → General). Leave, sick or holiday on one half only blocks
    that half: warnings, free-slot search and muted reminders follow it.
  - Titles of events and tasks are shown in red in their details.

3 DAYS VIEW
  The "3 days" button (or key 3) shows the selected day and the next two
  as a time grid; ‹ › move by 3 days.
